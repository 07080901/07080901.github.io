{%- comment -%} Turns a date written day/month/year (15/05/2026 or 15/5/2026) into 2026-05-15, which sorts correctly and works with Liquid's date filter. Old-style 2026-05-15 dates pass through unchanged. Param: date {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _dmy = include.date | split: '/' -%} {%- if _dmy.size == 3 -%} {{- _dmy[2] | strip -}}-{{- _dmy[1] | strip | prepend: '0' | slice: -2, 2 -}}-{{- _dmy[0] | strip | prepend: '0' | slice: -2, 2 -}} {%- else -%} {{- include.date | date: '%Y-%m-%d' -}} {%- endif -%}
