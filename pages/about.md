## Philosophy
**Making the guitar a mainstream instrument** and bringing **blues to my generation** are my most deeply felt goals.  

In the early 1980s the blues genre was falling out of fashion, it was considered by most outdated: during that same period many artists like Stevie Ray Vaughan, the same way as Jimi Hendrix did during the late 60s, **energized the blues** and made it **popular their his generation**.  
My mission is, using a completely new and different sound, to achieve the same effect for my generation: to **reinvent the blues genre one more time**, finding a way to make it reach young folks like myself.  
As countless musicians have noted to this day, the blues is **full of gems** to be discovered! Let's bring them to the public!
  
Regarding the current state of commercial music and pop culture: in **mainstream media** not only genres are mostly plain, uninspiring and lacking variation but also the guitar is not as widely considered as it used to be!  
Not to be mistaken with *not-so-known* music! Today, objectively, there are so many good artists that bring something very valuable to the table and most of the time they don't reach an audience as big as they deserve.  
This is entirely due to corporations controlling the media we consume, without letting quality material get out to everybody.

Analyzing instead smaller contexts, where corporations and record labeles do not count as much, genres that are considered *non-mainstream* are **widely appreciated** by the young public, also inside that reality the guitar is huge! This tells us that everyday people do like **authentic music**: that's what matters.  
I want to bring that same excitement, along with the presence of the guitar, to the everybody's ears.
Let's change what's considered pop nowadays. Let's make it real!
