## Why this one

Half of these songs were written with a soldering iron still warm on the desk. I was building my own fuzz pedal, and every time it buzzed, hummed or crackled, I wrote a riff around the noise.

The EP is the sound of a bedroom that is also a workshop: guitars through homemade circuits, with the hum left in on purpose.
